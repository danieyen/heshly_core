# Heshly Tenant Onboarding

## Objective

Provide a standardized process for onboarding new business tenants onto the Heshly platform.

---

## Prerequisites

- Business account approved
- Subscription active
- Domain available
- Administrator identified

---

## Onboarding Steps

1. Create tenant account.
2. Provision database.
3. Create administrator user.
4. Configure business profile.
5. Configure business hours.
6. Configure services.
7. Configure calendar integration.
8. Configure payment provider.
9. Configure AI receptionist.
10. Configure CRM pipeline.
11. Configure notifications.
12. Enable monitoring.
13. Run health checks.
14. Send welcome email.

---

## Verification Checklist

- Tenant created
- Administrator login successful
- Dashboard available
- Calendar working
- AI responding
- CRM operational
- Payments configured
- Notifications working

---

## Success Criteria

- Tenant fully provisioned
- All integrations operational
- Health checks passing
- Customer ready for production
